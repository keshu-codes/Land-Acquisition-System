National Land Acquisition & Management System (NLAMS)

Statutory Problem Statement #NLAMS-2026

Eliminating 3 to 7 Year Highway & Rail Land Acquisition Delays

In India, mega infrastructure projects lose over ₹1.2 Lakh Crores annually due to paper notice failures, manual surveyor routing bottlenecks, and valuation disputes under the RFCTLARR Act, 2013. NLAMS digitizes and compresses this 6-month cycle into under 72 hours.

72 Hrs Notice Cycle (vs 6 Mos)
100% Audit Transparency
0 Middlemen Friction
SHA-256 Deed Fraud Protection

1. Paper Notice Failures

Problem: Physical Section 11 notices take months to deliver. Landowners claim non-receipt and obtain court stay orders.

NLAMS Fix: TLS 1.3 encrypted email dispatch with 30-day single-use cryptographic JWT tokens.

2. Manual Surveyor Routing

Problem: District collectors manually look for surveyors through phone calls, causing weeks of inspection delays.

NLAMS Fix: Haversine geospatial proximity algorithm auto-selects the nearest active surveyor in seconds.

3. Compensation Corruption

Problem: Compensation passes through multiple bureaucratic accounts with middleman cuts and clerical delays.

NLAMS Fix: Web3 Smart Escrow Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) with PFMS cryptographic receipts.

Mentor Evaluation Defense Guide

Quick-Reference Cards for Round 1 & Round 2 Mentor Reviews

100% Prepared

Q1 "What is the statutory / legal foundation of this project?"

What to Say: "Our platform is built specifically under the statutory mandate of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013. We digitize Section 11 (Preliminary Acquisition Notification), Section 15 (Citizen Hearing of Objections), Section 19 (Declaration of Acquisition), and Section 23 (Enquiry and Land Award Determination)."

Q2 "How do you ensure rural citizens can file objections without complex logins?"

What to Say: "We use a Zero-Trust Tokenized Portal. When the Section 11 notice is dispatched, the citizen receives a 30-day cryptographic JWT link via email. They click it, view their pre-verified plot details, and submit their objection without needing to create or remember login passwords. Once submitted, the token is permanently burned to stop duplicate spam."

Q3 "How does your nearest surveyor algorithm work?"

What to Say: "We use the Haversine Formula to compute spherical trigonometry great-circle distance between the cadastral parcel GPS coordinates and all active district survey stations. The system sorts officers by distance and auto-selects the closest available officer (e.g., Rajesh Mohapatra at 2.2 km)."
