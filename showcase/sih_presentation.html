IDEA/APPROACH DETAILS

SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026
SIH

Proposed Solution:

Developed an automated national platform for statutory Land Acquisition under the RFCTLARR Act 2013:

  • Automated 5-Tier LARR Workflow: Enforces sequential milestones from Section 4 SIA to Section 23 Award finalization.
  • Cadastral GIS Boundary Triangulation: High-precision satellite & drone visual maps for sub-3m boundary verification.
  • Web3 Smart Contract Escrow: Automated Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) with 100% Solatium calculation.
  • Nearest Surveyor Dispatch: Computes nearest certified officer via Haversine distance algorithm.
  • Tokenized Citizen Objection Portal: 30-day TLS 1.3 secure link for zero-login objection submissions.
  • Mobile Field Station: Real device HTML5 GPS geo-fencing for ground revenue officers.
  • Interoperability Hub: Real-time REST API sync with BHOOMI, Bhunaksha, PM GatiShakti & PFMS.

How to addresses the problem

1. Eradication of Boundary Disputes: Satellite polygon overlay and GPS geo-fencing prevent overlapping claims and fake inspections.

2. 100% Transparency & Anti-Corruption: SHA-256 deed hashing and on-chain escrow guarantee funds reach genuine landowners directly.

3. Fast-Track Infrastructure: Accelerates acquisition timeline from 36 months to under 10 months.

Innovation and Uniqueness

  1. Automated 100% Solatium & Multiplier Engine under Section 30.
  2. Haversine-Based Nearest Surveyor Dispatch with 2FA verification.
  3. Real Device GPS Telemetry on field surveyor tablets.
  4. Single-Use Cryptographic Citizen Links (No password needed for farmers).
  5. ML Spatial Latency Predictor flagging bottleneck risks in corridors.
  6. Bilingual Accessibility (Hindi & English with 1-click search).
