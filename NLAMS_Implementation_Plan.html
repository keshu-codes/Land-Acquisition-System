1. Executive Summary & Vision

The National Land Acquisition & Management System (NLAMS) is an enterprise-grade, Web3-audited geospatial portal built for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026. It digitizes and accelerates the land acquisition lifecycle under the statutory provisions of the RFCTLARR Act, 2013.

  • 5-Tier Role-Based Governance: Central Ministry, State GIS Authorities, District Collectors, Field Surveyors, and Citizens.
  • Nearest Surveyor Assignment: Haversine proximity routing to assign officers and trigger Section 11 notices.
  • Citizen Objection Security: Single-use cryptographic tokens dispatched via TLS 1.3 encrypted emails (SPF/DKIM/DMARC compliant).
  • Web3 Spatial Escrow: On-chain immutable audit logs, PFMS compensation disbursement, and SHA-256 title deed validation.

3. End-to-End Technical Workflows

A. Automated Nearest Surveyor Dispatch & Section 11 Notice

  1. District Collector selects parcel (e.g. PLOT-OD-2026-9821).
  2. Haversine formula computes real-time distance across available district surveyors and auto-selects the closest officer (Rajesh Mohapatra, SO-774, 2.2 km).
  3. Collector confirms dispatch with security key (SIH@12345).
  4. Generates a 30-day cryptographic single-use JWT token (GRV-2026-9821-XXX).
  5. Dispatches official notification email via TLS 1.3 SMTP to anmol78953030@gmail.com.

B. Citizen Objection & Grievance Review

  1. Landowner clicks [ File Objection ] in their email.
  2. Public portal validates cryptographic token with zero login credentials required.
  3. Landowner submits dispute (Valuation / Boundary / Title).
  4. Token is marked as is_used = True (single-use enforced).
  5. Dispute appears instantly on the Executive Live Grievance Monitor.

C. Web3 Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) Escrow & Title Verification

  1. Awarded compensation is locked in digital Smart Escrow.
  2. Landowner connects Web3 wallet and claims compensation.
  3. Generates official PFMS cryptographic payout advice receipt with transaction hash and block number.
  4. Land deeds are verified via client-side SHA-256 hash matching against the on-chain registry.
